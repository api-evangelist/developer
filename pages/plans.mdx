**Discovery is free.** The research *depth* — full content, curated guides, and catalog
intelligence — is **Pro**.

## Free

Everything you need to discover and navigate the network, keyless:

- **Search & browse** every content type — 5,250+ stories, 77 topic areas, 2,600+ governance
  building blocks, papers, conversations, videos, solutions, tools, and vocabulary.
- **Summaries & metadata** for every item (title, summary, tags, links).
- **Tag taxonomy** — `/tags` and `/tags/{slug}`: the map of everything API Evangelist covers.
- **Related content** — `/{type}/{slug}/related`: the shared-tag relevance graph across the network.
- **Insights discovery** — `/insights/dimensions`, `/insights/adoption`, `/insights/companies`: the demand-side view of what 6,572 companies build, buy, and hire for.
- **Demand entity catalog** — `/insights/entities`: every service, tool, and standard companies are
  measured against, ranked by reach. Each response states the corpus, the matcher, and the
  truncation that bounds it.
- **Standards measurement** — `/standards/measured` and `/standards/{slug}/adoption`, `/coalition`,
  `/repositories`: what a standards body actually ships — repositories published, how many graduated,
  who governs it, which companies pay for membership — for the 14 bodies profiled so far. Free on
  purpose: a specification is a public good, so an honest read of its adoption should not sit behind
  a paywall. Every response names what is **not** measured rather than implying zero.
- **Contact** — every way to engage.

Rate limits: 5 req/s, 1,000 req/day. No key required.

## Pro

The research layer — for teams and agents that need the depth, not just the map:

| Capability | Endpoint / tool | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Full research content** | `?include=content` on any item | The complete markdown bodies and transcripts — the actual research, ready to quote and synthesize. |
| **Curated topic guides** | `/guide?topic=` · `guide_topic` | One call bundles the topic area, top guidance/rules/policies/standards, papers, recent stories, and the services that can help. |
| **Catalog intelligence** | `/stats` · `get_stats` | Per-type counts, stories-per-year, and the top tags by reach across the network. |
| **Insights company depth** | `/insights/company/{slug}` · `get_company_insight` · `company_gaps` | The full 40-dimension demand-side profile of a Fortune 1000 / provider company, its adopted stack, and where it's thin. (Discovery — `/insights/dimensions`, `/adoption`, `/companies` — stays free.) |
| **Benchmarked dimensions** | `/insights/company/{slug}/dimensions` · `company_dimensions` | Every dimension read against the measured cohort — median, mean, max, rank, percentile, and an index where 1.0 is exactly typical. A raw score of 26 is unreadable; these are the columns that make it mean something. |
| **Peers & whitespace** | `/insights/company/{slug}/peers` · `/whitespace` · `/signals` | The companies most comparable to this one, what they run that it does not, and its own stack entity by entity with each entity's reach across the cohort. |
| **The demand inversion** | `/insights/entity/{kind}/{slug}` · `entity_demand` | Which companies show demand for a given service, tool, or standard, broken down by industry. Every other endpoint runs company → stack; this one runs stack → companies. It is the prospecting query, and it exists in no report or bundle. |

Pro also unlocks the paid **MCP prompts** — every prompt marked Pro in `prompts/list`, from
`research_topic` to `fix_these_diagnostics` — and the `ae://stats` / `ae://guide/{topic}` MCP
resources.

Every Pro capability above is available as both a REST endpoint and an MCP tool — the MCP is a
pass-through over the same API, so the two return identical results and there is one paywall, not
two.

### Getting started with Pro

Self-serve — no email required:

1. **Sign in** to create your account and a free-tier key —
   [GitHub](https://api.apievangelist.com/v1/auth/login?provider=github) ·
   [Google](https://api.apievangelist.com/v1/auth/login?provider=google) ·
   [LinkedIn](https://api.apievangelist.com/v1/auth/login?provider=linkedin).
   A second provider on the same verified email joins the account you already have.
2. **[Upgrade to Pro or Team](/account)** via Stripe checkout — your key is moved onto the
   paid usage plan automatically when checkout completes.

Send your key in the `x-api-key` header:

```bash
curl "https://api.apievangelist.com/v1/guide?topic=api%20governance" \
  -H "x-api-key: YOUR_PRO_KEY"
```

Prefer a hand-set-up account or volume/annual pricing? Email
[info@apievangelist.com](mailto:info@apievangelist.com) and Kin Lane will sort it out.
